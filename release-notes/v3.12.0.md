### What's new in 3.12

## A new look, laid out like Nyarch Assistant

- **Three panes.** History on the left (its ☰ holds Temporary chat, search, Live, Characters, Images & Videos, Reverse Engineering, Habitat and Settings; the blue New Chat button at the bottom), the chat in the middle, and a panel on the right.
- **The chat.** A model button in the middle of the top bar ("Ithil · her own mind", "Team · Claude leads · repo"), with ⚙ for the chat's options; a yellow line saying what the AIs here can reach; every message as a row with "You:" or the AI's name in its colour; **Thoughts** boxes you can open (Claude's and ChatGPT's real thinking); **Clear** (with Undo), **Regenerate** and **Stop the team**; and a new input bar with ⚙, 📎 and blue microphone and send buttons.
- **The right panel.** Ithil's full body from Hearth AI (or whoever is answering), with read-aloud, who stands there, and the window's own buttons. ◫ switches to **Files, Terminal, Browser, Changes and Ithil**.
- **The mini window.** Ithil and a short talk with her, over whatever you're doing: **Super + I**.
- **Settings** is now a dialog with seven tabs: AI team (every AI with its switch), Accounts (grouped by how each AI connects), Ithil, Tools, Memory, Voice & avatar and General. Everything from before is still there, under "More … settings".
- **Look › Style › Dark** with an **Accent colour** is the new default. If you picked Gold hologram or Classic before, that's kept until you change it.
- New switches: Live previews, remembering things between chats, a notice when a task finishes and on your phone, downloading updates by themselves, and your home server's AI in chats. Ithil: handing her tasks, her Mesh station, and showing her on Cores.
