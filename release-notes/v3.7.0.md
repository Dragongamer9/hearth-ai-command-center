### What's new in 3.7

## Live › Mesh

- **Live opens on the Mesh.** The AIs working now sit in the middle, and around them are the stations they use: Files, Search, Shell, Checks, Web, Git, Helpers, Tools, Make, Memory, You and Replies. A station lights up in the colour of the AI using it, with a line and a pulse from that AI, and each one shows this chat's count and what was done last ("Edited BatteryWatch.qml", "Read upower.freedesktop.org"). **Cores** at the top right switches back to the old view, and the app remembers which one you picked.

## Talk sounds sooner and more natural

- **Replies are spoken sentence by sentence.** The first sentence goes to the voice as soon as the reply lands, and the next ones are made while it speaks. Talk no longer waits for the whole team to finish: in the same test, the first sound came after 9.5 s instead of 12.3 s with the lead alone, and after 9.4 s instead of 32.3 s when a teammate checked the reply.
- **Tables, code blocks, links and Markdown symbols aren't read aloud.** Links say only their words, and lists are read as separate sentences.
- **The AIs know you're talking.** A message you said out loud tells them their reply will be read to you, so they write it to be heard. There's no limit on how long the reply can be.
- **Talk to any AI by name.** Say "Gemini, …", "Hey Codex …" or "Claude and Grok: …" and they answer instead of only the lead.
- **Each AI can pick its own voice.** When replies are read aloud, an AI may choose its voice from the list. You can change it any time in Settings › Voice, and your choice always wins.
- **How long it took.** Talk shows the time from the end of your words to the first sound of the reply, after every turn.

## Speech to text

- **Install speech to text beside the mic.** If nothing on this computer can turn voice notes into text, an Install button sets it up in the app's own folder, with no password or terminal.
- **Your PC's Speech to text is used when this computer has none.** Voice notes go to the whisper on your Hearth devices.
- **The most accurate whisper model you've downloaded is used.** Before, base.en was picked even with a better one beside it.
