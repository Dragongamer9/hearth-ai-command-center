### What's new in 2.9

## A new look: the gold hologram

- **The whole app is redesigned** in a dark gold, holographic style: glowing panels with corner marks, a dotted grid behind everything, and wide capital headings.
- **Prefer the old look?** Settings › Appearance › Design › **Classic** brings it back, along with your theme and Hearth OS colours. Nothing else changes.

## Live: every AI as a living core

- **A new Live section** at the top of the sidebar, and a **Live** button on every chat. Every AI is there as its own animated core, each with its own shape and colour: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Free AI, Grok, Mistral, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Qwen, FreeBuff, Venice, Civitai, your GPU server, Hearth devices and Local AI, plus ComfyUI, Voices and Listening from your PCs.
- **Each AI has its own talking motion.** The AI that's working or speaking moves in its own way and sends out rings in its colour. Its newest line shows in the bar at the top.
- **Click a core to hear it.** It reads that AI's newest line aloud in its voice. The mic button opens Talk with the whole team.
- **The chat sits on the right** and hides away with **Hide chat**. Your message box stays at the bottom, and **Full chat** opens the usual chat view.
- **Reduce motion** (Settings › Appearance) stops every animation.

## FreeBuff on the web

- **Settings › Accounts › FreeBuff on the web › Open FreeBuff** opens freebuff.com in its own window. Sign in there once; it stays signed in. Your FreeBuff chats live in FreeBuff's cloud, so the ones from your phone are there too. Clicking FreeBuff's core opens it as well.
- FreeBuff has no official way for other apps to drive it yet, so it can't join team chats. The Command Center never reads or copies your FreeBuff login.
