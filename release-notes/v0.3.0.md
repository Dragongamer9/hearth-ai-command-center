### What's new in 0.3.0
- **Connect account, no API keys:** each AI connects through its provider's own official sign-in. Claude uses Claude Code, ChatGPT uses Codex ("Sign in with ChatGPT"), and Gemini uses Gemini CLI ("Sign in with Google"). Hearth AI never sees your passwords or login tokens. **Disconnect** signs out through the same official tool.
- **New teammates:** Gemini (free tier with a Google account) and a free **Local AI** via Ollama that never runs out of usage.
- **Paid by:** every AI shows whether it uses your subscription, your plan, a free tier, or API billing.
- **Control Room:** who's connected, available, out of usage, and what each AI is working on.
- **Claude leads the team:** every AI is told what the Command Center can do, who's on the team and what's on the board. Claude answers first and splits up the work. **Add & start** turns a proposed task into running work in one click.
- **Setup checklist** on first run, **Show changes** on tasks, and a **Use** on/off switch per AI.
