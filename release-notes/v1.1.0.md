### What's new in 1.1

**A 3D Habitat: the Cozy Hearth lodge**
- Switch the Habitat to **3D** to see the team's timber lodge on its hill, cut away like a dollhouse: each AI's room in its colour, the great room with a desk per AI and a crackling stone fireplace, the kitchen nook, the hall with the board and trophies, and the garden outside.
- The AIs are little 3D people with faces and moods: they walk to their desks, type, grab coffee, carry work to the reviewer, wave, cheer and sleep, just like in 2D.
- Day and night follow your clock, with glowing windows at night and snow or rain outside. Orbit, zoom and jump to any room.
- **Walk the lodge yourself** as your own character, in third or first person, with keyboard and mouse, a controller or touch. Talk to an AI to see its task, read and pin notes, pet the fox, sit by the fire.
- **Your private study:** a room only you can enter. Decorate it yourself, with a wall screen showing the live overview. The AIs can't go in or change anything there.
- 2D stays as it was; there's a Lighter 3D option for slower computers, and it falls back to 2D if 3D isn't available.

**The team works more reliably**
- **A truth card on every task,** straight from git: its branch, commit, how far behind main, test result, the reviewer's last verdict and where it's merged.
- **Every action line gets ✓ or ✗ with the reason.** Approvals no longer get lost when a task is stopped.
- **The task's instructions reach the AI working on it,** and it has to acknowledge each one.
- **Every run starts from up-to-date code,** with missing tools shown as blockers instead of looking like broken code.
- **Verify-only runs** that check and report without changing anything.
- **The review lock:** once work is submitted the implementer waits, and an approval sticks to the exact commit that was reviewed.
- **One review ping** with everything the reviewer needs.
- **A release gate:** before any release, a preview and checklist (your version, notes that match what's merged, all tests passing, your name on every commit). Only you can override it. You're warned if a tag or release appears that the app didn't make.
- A docs and research mode, generated images attached automatically, a Jobs panel, full image and video details, and Habitat status in the team panel.

**Fixes**
- The phone view no longer closes the keyboard while you type, and Enter sends.
- In-app updates (AppImage and .deb) are protected by a check that fails the build if they ever break.

AppImage: update from inside the app (Settings → App updates → Check for updates).

.deb installs update from inside the app too (asks for your computer password once per update).
