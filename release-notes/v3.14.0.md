### What's new in 3.14

## Everything connects by itself

- **Your Hearth devices are found by themselves.** No address, port or share code to type: the Command Center looks at
  your own Tailscale devices (the ones signed in to your Tailscale account) and asks each one which AI it shares (chat,
  pictures, voices, speech to text), the Nvidia PC first. It looks when it starts and every few minutes.
- **A saved address that stops working doesn't stop you.** If the Home server address in Settings › Accounts ›
  Hearth devices doesn't answer (it's off, or the port is wrong), your devices are found on Tailscale instead, and
  Settings says so. Your address and your device order stay exactly as you set them, and they still come first.
- **Your keys work on every desktop.** The Command Center now remembers which password store your keys were saved in
  (KDE's wallet or GNOME Keyring) and always opens that one, so switching between KDE Plasma and Mantel no longer
  makes your GPU servers, keys and sign-ins look missing. Keys saved before 3.14 on another desktop are found by
  itself: the app starts itself once more with the other store, and never again after that.
- Nothing found is ever saved over your settings. In-app updates work as before, for the AppImage and the .deb.

## The Claude app look

- **A new look, like the Claude app**, and the new default: warm dark or light colours, answers in an easy-to-read
  serif font, and the sidebar always showing Chats, Repos, Images & Videos, Live, Habitat, Reverse Engineering, Agent
  Desktop and Characters, with Starred and Recents. If you picked Gold hologram or Classic, you keep it; Settings ›
  Appearance › Style switches back any time.
- **A new message box**: + to attach, tools, the repo, the team with its little cores, the mic and ↑ send. Pick
  **who works on this chat** with a switch for each AI (kept with that chat).
- **Chats**: your messages in a bubble, tool steps as neat one-line rows, 👍 / 👎 under answers, and a **Ready to
  merge** card with Approve & merge, Look at the changes and Not yet. The side panel has Changes, Code and Preview.
- **Settings as a full page** with General, Appearance, Accounts, GitHub, Team, Voice, Memory, Images & Videos, Phone,
  Updates and Credits. Appearance has Colour mode (Light / Dark / Match Hearth), Chat font (Serif / Sans / Dyslexic
  friendly), Send with Enter and cores in the message box.
- **Images & Videos**: **Edit a picture** (say what to change and how much), **Starred**, and **Open gallery**.
- **Agent Desktop has its own page**: the screen your AI is using with what it wants to click marked, and Allow /
  Allow for this task / Stop. The screen picture is shown only to you, never sent to the AI.
- **Live** opens calm: the team as glowing cores (talking / listening / thinking / idle) and what's being said.
- **Habitat**: Great hall, King's chambers, Workroom and 2D ⇄ 3D at the top, each AI's name and what it's doing
  above its head, and Habitat journal, Board and Wave buttons.
- **Reverse Engineering**: each project as a card above its REA chat.
