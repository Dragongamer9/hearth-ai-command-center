### What's new in 3.15

## Only the Claude app look

- **The Command Center now always looks like the Claude app** (the owner: "BY default i want it to look like the
  claude app, and only the claude app"). The gold hologram, Dark and Classic looks are gone, and so is the Style
  choice in Settings. Whatever you had picked before, the app opens in the Claude app look.
- **Colour mode** takes the Style choice's place in Settings › General and Appearance: Light, Dark, or Match Hearth
  (follows Hearth OS). Your other settings stay exactly as they are.
- Fixed: picking "Claude app" in 3.14's Style choice didn't stick; it went back to the gold look.
- In-app updates work as before.
