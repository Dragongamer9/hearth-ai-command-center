### What's new in 2.8

## Reverse Engineering

- **A new section in the sidebar.** Press **New project** and pick a program: a Windows .exe or .dll, a Linux program, an .apk, an Electron .asar, a .jar, firmware or a capture. It gets its own chat, where your AI looks inside it with REA (Reverse Engineer Anything, MIT). It tells you what the program is, what it's built with and what each part does, with the evidence for each answer.
- **REA is off until you press Turn on,** and only these project chats get it. Its own setup is never run, so Claude Code's, Codex's and Gemini's settings stay as they are.
- **The first message is ready in your message box.** Nothing is sent until you press Send.
- **What it needs:** Node.js 22.23 or newer. For Windows and Linux programs it also needs Ghidra 12.1 or newer (free) and Java 21 or newer. The section shows what's found, and you pick your Ghidra folder there.
- **Use it only on software you own or are allowed to look at.**

## Let your AI use this desktop

- **A new switch** in Settings › Connectors, off until you turn it on. Claude Code, Codex and Gemini can then look at your open apps and work them for you, with Agent Desktop (Apache-2.0).
- **Every action asks you first.** A card shows what it wants to click, type, press, open or close. Choose **Allow**, **Allow for 10 minutes in this chat**, or **Don't allow**. No answer in 2 minutes is a no. Looking doesn't ask.
- **On Hearth's desktop** it clicks, types and picks options through the apps themselves. Moving the mouse pointer and pressing keys for you comes with a later Hearth update.
