### What's new in 3.8

## Electric currents on the Mesh

- **Live › Mesh crackles.** Jagged electric sparks run from each AI to whatever it's doing (Files, Shell, Web, Git…), in that AI's colour, flickering a few times a second. If your system asks for less motion, a single still spark is shown instead.

## Your voices

- **One speaking speed for everyone.** Settings › Voice › Speaking speed, from 0.75× to 2×. A voice with its own speed, or a chat's Voice button, still keeps its own.
- **Hearth Voice Studio is a ready-made speech server.** Settings › Voice › Speech servers › Add › Hearth Voice Studio, with its port filled in. Paste its API key from Settings › Home server › Voice Studio on your PC, and every voice you made there can be picked, by you and by the AIs.
- **New voices show up straight away.** Speech servers are asked again every time you open Settings › Voice or Talk (the list was kept for 5 minutes).

## Speech to text: Fast or Most accurate

- **Fast is back as the default.** 3.7 always used your biggest whisper model, which is slow on a laptop's processor. Settings › Voice › whisper on this PC now shows the model in use and lets you pick Fast, Most accurate, or one model.
- **Quicker on every PC.** whisper uses all of the processor's threads (it used 4), and Fast makes one guess instead of five.
- **whisper.cpp's test files are skipped.** The for-tests models that come with whisper.cpp are never used.
