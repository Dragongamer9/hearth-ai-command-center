### What's new in 2.2.1

## Images & Videos

There's a new **Images & Videos** page in the sidebar. You ask for a picture or a video and your Nvidia PC makes it with ComfyUI, over Tailscale. It uses the ComfyUI address from Settings › Accounts › ComfyUI server (like `http://chris-hearth.tail0e2082.ts.net:8188`).

- **Your PC at the top:** whether it's online, its graphics card and how much graphics memory is free. Press the refresh button to check again.
- **Picture or Video:** write what it should show. Pictures let you pick the size, the model and how many (1, 2 or 4). **More options** has words to leave out, the seed and the steps.
- **Videos use Wan 2.2 14B,** the model `install-wan22-video.sh` put on your PC. The app sends the whole setup to ComfyUI itself, so there's nothing to wire up there. A video is about 5 seconds at 832 × 480 (wide) or 480 × 832 (tall), made in 4 steps.
  - **Text to video** uses the T2V files. If you ran the script with `--i2v-only`, the page says so and offers to animate a picture instead.
  - **Picture to video:** pick a picture from **Made so far** (or press **Animate** on one), or a picture file from your computer.
  - If something is missing on the PC (the GGUF node, a model file, the LoRA), it says which one and to run the script.
- **On your PC now:** what's being made and what's waiting, with how long it's taken and roughly how long it should take (after your first one of each kind). **Stop** stops it on the PC; **Remove** takes a waiting one out.
- **Made so far:** everything you've made, newest first, with a filter for pictures or videos. Each one has **Save**, **Send to a chat** (it goes into the message box of your last chat), **Again** (same request, new seed) and delete. They stay until you delete them, and they're saved like your chats (written in one step, with a backup, never written over when the file can't be read).

## Uncensored mode (18+)

A new page in **Settings › Uncensored mode**, with one switch. It's off until you turn it on, and the first time it asks you to confirm you're 18 or older. It stays exactly as you set it.

When it's on:

- **Videos** use the uncensored Wan 2.2 LoRA pair (Wan2.2_LightX2V_4Step_Uncensored).
- **Pictures** no longer leave out nudity, and can use an 18+ picture model you pick on that page.
- **Local chat** (Local AI and Hearth devices) answers with the 18+ chat model you pick there, for example one of Hearth's abliterated Qwen3 models or Dolphin 3.
- **Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini don't change.** They keep their own rules, and pictures and videos made in Uncensored mode are never sent to them (**Send to a chat** is off for those).
- **18+ pictures and videos are blurred** in Images & Videos until you click them, and they're marked 18+.

**The hard limits stay on in every mode, and no setting changes them:**

- nothing sexual involving minors or anyone who looks under 18;
- no sexual or nude pictures or videos of real people, and no real person's likeness;
- a sexual video can only start from a picture made in Images & Videos, never from a photo you add;
- minors are left out of every picture and video.

A request that breaks one of these is refused with the reason, and it never reaches your PC.

## Each chat keeps its own settings

Who a chat talks to (the whole team, or just one AI like Grok), **Web**, **Run & build** and **Deep research** now belong to that chat.

- **Switching chats brings back that chat's own choices.** A chat set to talk only to Grok stays that way, and the next chat you open isn't changed by it.
- **A new chat starts from your defaults** (Settings), never from whatever the last chat used.
- **Saved with the chat,** in its own file (written in one step, with a backup, never written over when it can't be read), and only once you change something in it. Older chats use your defaults until you do.
- Each chat's picked models (Choose models) already stayed with the chat; the models you pick on the home screen now go only to the chat you start there.
- Uncensored mode stays one switch for the whole app (Settings), as before.

AppImage: update from inside the app (Settings → App updates → Check for updates).

.deb installs update from inside the app too (asks for your computer password once per update).
