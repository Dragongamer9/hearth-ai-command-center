### What's new in 1.2

**New look**
- A calmer, roomier design in warm light and warm dark, with the ember colour from the Hearth flame. Headings use Lora and body text Inter; both fonts ship inside the app.
- Home replaces Overview: a greeting, the message box in the middle, quick buttons (plan a feature, fix a bug from sent logs, review pull requests, team check-in, prepare a release) and a "While you were away" card. The old overview is still there under "Team at a glance".
- A slimmer sidebar: New task, Team chat, Board, Approvals, Jobs, Habitat and a More menu with every other page. Below that: your projects, recent tasks, and the Team list with a dot for each AI (the lead has a ★). Settings and the Update button sit at the bottom next to your name.
- Team chat reads like one conversation in the middle of the window. Your messages sit in soft cards, each AI has a coloured letter, and ✓/✗ results sit in a thin line under the message. Approvals and permission requests show as cards right in the chat.
- One big message box: attach, pick who it goes to (or write @name), choose Routine or Major (Major adds a note asking the team to check with you first), voice note, and send.
- A side panel you can open beside the chat with tabs for Board, Task (the task's truth card, actions and history), Habitat (2D or 3D), Jobs, Approvals and Release.
- Settings is one page with a list of sections on the left, including App updates.
- The phone page has the same look: team chips, chat, big approval buttons and a tab bar at the bottom.
- Following the Hearth OS desktop theme still works, and none of your settings changed.

**Habitat**
- Each turn's presence line now starts with you: "You (name): place (status)", so the team always sees where you are.

AppImage: update from inside the app (Settings → App updates → Check for updates).

.deb installs update from inside the app too (asks for your computer password once per update).
