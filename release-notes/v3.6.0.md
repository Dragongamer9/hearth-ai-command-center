### What's new in 3.6

## A voice for each chat

- **Voice button in every chat's top bar.** Pick where this chat's voices come from (your main voice provider or one of your speech servers, like Hearth's Voice Studio), the voice the AI teammates speak with, and the speed (0.5× to 2×, for everyone in the chat). **Hear it** plays a sample, and **Back to my default** goes back to Settings › Voice. The choice is kept with the chat, and the button shows ✓ when a chat has its own voice. Roleplay characters keep their own voices.

## The page stays where you are

- **Clicking anything no longer sends a page back to the top.** Settings lists, panels and the side list keep their place when something changes.
- **Chats don't jump to the bottom when you click something.** A chat still goes to the bottom when you open it or send a message, and follows new replies only when you're already at the bottom.

## Fix

- **API credit is counted right again.** Only runs that really used your API key are counted (it showed about $241 for about $10 of use).
