### What's new in 0.8.0
- **Habitat:** a cozy pixel world where you can watch your AI team. Each AI has its own room in its color, and a desk in the shared workroom.
- **They act out what's really happening:** an AI walks to its desk and sits when it starts a task (the monitor shows the task), gets a speech bubble while typing in team chat, a magnifying glass while reviewing, sleeps when it's out of usage, and its door is locked when it isn't signed in. The lead wears a star, and a "?" means it's waiting for your answer.
- **Click or hover** any AI or desk to see the task and its branch, or jump to it.
- **Reduce motion:** a new setting under Settings → Appearance (it also follows your computer's own setting) keeps everyone still.
- All the pixel art is original and drawn by the app itself; nothing is downloaded.

AppImage: update from inside the app (Settings → App updates → Check for updates). The .deb updates when you install the newer .deb.
