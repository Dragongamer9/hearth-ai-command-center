### What's new in 3.5

## Team fixes (from testing every teammate in one chat)

- **No more silent teammates.** Every teammate you or the lead @-name that can't answer is now listed in the turn and named once in the chat, with what it needs ("Kiro: CLI missing, install it in Settings › Accounts") and a button to fix it: Install, Sign in, Set up, or Add to the team. The lead's summary lists them as not reached too.
- **The lead knows who's connected.** Every turn, the lead gets the team with each one's status (connected, not signed in, needs a key…), so it doesn't hand work to teammates that can't answer.
- **Website teammates in big rounds.** At most two web sign-in pages work at once, a page that reloads before the message is sent is tried again (a message is never sent twice), and closing a website window only hides it. When one times out, it says whether the page loaded, the chat box was found and the message was sent.
- **Sign-in and page problems are told apart**, and each error has an Open page / Sign in / Install button right in it.
- **Looks right / Not right** on Venice's and your GPU server's replies: the lead learns your verdict, never the content.
- **Qwen's answers are read again** (newer Qwen Code versions answer in a different format).
- **@GitHub Copilot, @Local AI and @Factory Droid** work with the space.

## New: Test team

- **Settings › AI team › Test team** (also in the Team menu): every connected teammate is asked to reply with its own check word, and you get one table of passed, failed (with the reason), not signed in, not connected, not in the team and skipped. Website teammates take turns, two at a time.

## Smaller and tidier

- **Draw the app at 100%** (Settings › Appearance): when your desktop scales the screen (125%, 150%…), the app can ignore that scale and look like the rest of the desktop.
- **App size** now goes down to 67%, and Ctrl −, Ctrl + and Ctrl 0 change it.
- **Compact team lists:** the Who answers menu, the AI team page (grouped cards that fold), the home team row and the model list all fit on the screen.
- Pasting a key is more forgiving (stray spaces, line breaks or quotes are dropped), and the Claude API credit card shows the key as soon as it's saved.
