### What's new in 3.0

## Your AI team works at the same time

- **The lead plans first.** It works out what your message needs and gives each teammate that would really help its own part, picked by what it's good at: code and edits, tests and big files, web research, writing. Teammates that wouldn't add anything are left out.
- **Everyone it picks works at once,** each in its own copy of the files, so they never trip over each other.
- **As soon as one is done, it reports to the lead and the lead answers right away,** while the others keep working: what's good, what's wrong, or more to do. Nothing waits for you.
- **When everyone is done, their changes are merged** into the chat's branch, as you (no AI names in the commits). If two teammates changed the same lines, that part isn't merged; it's kept on that teammate's own branch and the lead is told where. Then the lead checks it all and gives you one short answer: what was done, by whom, and what's left.
- **Settings › AI team › Work at the same time** is on. Turn it off to go back to one after another.

## See who's working

- **The AIs working right now are stacked** next to the chat's name, in the chat list and on Live. Each one leaves the stack when it's done.
- **Every working AI has its own card** in the chat, and on Live their cores all move at once.
- **A smaller chat window** in the gold design: narrower, with smaller text and a smaller message box.
