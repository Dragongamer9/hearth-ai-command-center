### What's new in 2.4

## The 3D Habitat is now an anime castle city

- **A new look.** Everything in 3D is drawn in an anime style: flat cel shading with clean ink outlines.
- **A castle city instead of the lodge.**
  - A city wall rings the town, with round towers and a gatehouse.
  - Inside are streets of houses and a market with stalls and a well.
  - Outside are forest, a river and fields.
  - The keep stands on its hill in the middle, inside its own walled ring with towers.
- **Every AI has its own house.** Each house carries the AI's name on a sign. You can decorate the inside, and the roof lifts off when you look in.
- **A real day for your team.**
  - Your AIs walk through town to the keep's great hall to work, and take tea by the fireplace when they're on a break.
  - At night they walk home to sleep.
  - It's the same team and the same simulation as before, only now they live in a city.
- **The King's Chambers are yours.** Your character's room sits at the top of the keep, in place of "your study". When you walk the castle, you start there.
- **Day, sunset and night** follow your clock. You can also set the time yourself from the Habitat's toolbar.
- **Lighter 3D and reduced motion** still work, and the city keeps to the same performance limits.
- The 2D Habitat hasn't changed.

## Characters

- **Anime chibi characters.** Each has a hair style (soft, bob, long, twin tails, ponytail or spiky), hair, eye and skin colours, an outfit, an accessory (glasses, cat ears, headphones or a ribbon) and an expression.
- **A new Characters page** (Habitat › Characters…) has 12 ready-made originals: Ren, Kaede, Miko, Sable, Tomo, Juno, Hoshi, Riku, Nao, Lumi, Aster and Pip. Pick **Give to…** to hand one to any AI, or to yourself.
- **Make your own** in the creator. It shows a live preview, and you can give the character a name, choose who it's for, try **Surprise me** and **Save**.
- All characters are originals. Your choices are saved with your other Habitat settings and never change on their own.

## The city is alive

- **Guards.**
  - They patrol the wall walks between the towers and stand watch at the gatehouse and the keep gate.
  - The watch changes at dusk, and at night they carry torches.
- **Townsfolk.**
  - Merchants at their stalls, children, a baker, a farmer heading out to the fields, neighbours stopping for a chat, and cats.
  - They walk between the houses, the market and the well, head home in the evening and come back out in the morning.
- They're scenery only and have nothing to do with your AIs' work.
- There are fewer of them in Lighter 3D. Turn them off with **Townsfolk and guards** in the Habitat's toolbar (it starts on).

## Raids

- **A few times a day, a band of 4 to 8 cartoon raiders marches up the road to the front gate.**
  - Archers on the walls shoot arrows and crossbow bolts over the gate, and guards at the gate fight with swords and spears.
  - Everyone in the fight has a floating health bar.
  - It's all family-friendly. A hit only flashes and knocks back. A beaten raider gets dizzy stars and runs off or vanishes in a poof, and a knocked-out guard sits dazed for a moment, then gets back up.
  - Raiders never get past the gate, and a raid is over in a minute or two.
- **Your AIs can help.** An AI that's free or on a break may join the defense. Work is never interrupted: an AI that gets a task leaves the gate at once.
- **Each AI's raiders-defeated count** shows on its tooltip and on the Characters page. It's kept with the Habitat and never resets on its own.
- **When a raid starts, you get a "Raiders at the gate!" notice.** Press **Watch** to jump to the gate.
- **Choose how often raids happen** with the raids menu in the Habitat's toolbar: off, rare, sometimes or often. It starts at sometimes.
- In Lighter 3D or with reduced motion, raids stay off unless you turn them on.

Everything else from 2.3 is the same. App updates work as before for both the AppImage and the .deb.
