### What's new in 3.11

## Talking to Ithil works from every device

- **Her own chat reaches her whenever she's connected.** It used to say "The AI you picked isn't ready" unless she was on the team and awake and free. Now a message goes to her even if she isn't on the team, or is asleep, resting or busy. Handing her a task still waits until she's free.
- **When she really can't answer, the chat says why:** for example that Hearth AI isn't running on the PC, or that the key wasn't accepted.
- **The host box understands a full address.** In Settings › Accounts › Ithil (Hearth AI) you can type `http://100.72.99.97:47860` or just `100.72.99.97`; both work.
