### What's new in 3.1

## The web sign-in bridge

- **Use your own account instead of paid API keys** for Perplexity, DeepSeek, Venice and FreeBuff: Settings › Accounts › Web sign-in bridge. Press **Open**, sign in on the site as usual, close the window, and turn the switch on.
- **How it works:** when the lead hands that AI work, the Command Center asks it through its own website in a hidden window, signed in as you: it types the question, waits for the answer to finish and reads it back to the team. One question at a time, at a person's pace. Text only: it can't read or change your files.
- **Your sign-in stays in that site's own window.** Nothing is read from it, copied or sent anywhere else.
- **Every switch starts off.** Turning one on warns you first: it's against that site's terms, and your account could be suspended. While it's on, it's used instead of that AI's API key.
- **FreeBuff can now join team chats** through the bridge.
- Websites change: if one stops answering through the bridge, it may need an app update.

## Also

- **Mistral** already signs in with your Mistral account: Settings › Accounts › Mistral › Sign in, then choose **Launch browser**.
