### What's new in 2.7

## Free AI

- **A new teammate that uses free AI.** Settings › Accounts › Free AI takes a free key for each provider you want: Cerebras, Groq, Google AI Studio, Mistral and OpenRouter (its free models only). Each one has a "Get a key" link.
- **Never stuck at a limit.** Free AI tries them in order. When one is at its free limit it waits, and the next one answers. Each reply says which provider and model answered.
- **Nothing to type.** If a provider drops a model, Free AI picks another from that provider's own list.
- **Your keys** are saved encrypted, and each only ever goes to its own provider.
- **Using it:** Free AI is in Settings › AI team, and you can @Free it in a chat. Based on FreeLLMAPI (MIT).

## Make it sound human

- **A new person button** on your message box and on every AI reply rewrites the text so it reads less like AI, using Humanizer's rules (MIT). The facts stay the same.
- **You pick the AI** that does the rewrite, with Free AI first.
- **Nothing is sent until you press Send.** The rewrite goes into your message box.
- **Uncensored replies:** a reply from Venice, your GPU server or Hearth devices is only rewritten by one of those three.
