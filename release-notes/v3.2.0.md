### What's new in 3.2

## See everyone working, on the left

- **Under a chat that's working, a small list shows everyone in it right now:** each AI and what it's doing (reading files, leading, working in its own copy), with a tiny moving core in the gold design.
- **The lead's side helpers** show there too, with what each is helping with, and **an AI's own helpers** (like the ones Claude starts herself) as a count.
- **Each one leaves the list when it's done.** The stack next to the chat's name now shows even when only one AI is working.
