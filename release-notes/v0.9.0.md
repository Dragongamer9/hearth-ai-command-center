### What's new in 0.9.0

**The team needs you less**
- **The app runs the tests itself** when an AI says it's done, at that exact commit. A failing test means it isn't done.
- **While you were away:** a summary of what each AI did, what's waiting for you and what failed.
- **Budget aware:** work goes to the AI with the most usage left, with a warning before one runs out.
- **Automatic hand-off:** if an AI crashes, stalls or runs out, another AI with the same role picks the task up from its last checkpoint (up to twice, then it asks you).
- **CI watching:** a failed GitHub test goes straight back to the AI that wrote the code, with the log.
- **Claude gets a turn by itself** when a card, a review or a task is waiting for it, and sees exactly what's waiting.

**You stay in control**
- **Approval levels:** routine things (posting images, running tests, editing their own branch) just happen and are logged; major things ask you or Claude; settings and version numbers are always yours.
- **New lead switches, all off until you turn them on:** Claude may merge reviewed work to main, publish a release and create its tag, but only for the version you set in "Next version".
- **Every commit under your name:** set your owner identity once; attribution lines are stripped and every push, merge, release and tag is checked first.

**Working together**
- Every action line in team chat gets a ✓ or the reason it didn't work, and there's a Board actions log.
- `@local` works, and unknown names are pointed out.
- Each AI has its own assets folder, kept out of git; generated images land there.
- A capability panel shows what each AI can really do.
- Progress updates fold away; attachments show their size and are flagged when they're smaller than asked.
- Upscale images (Real-ESRGAN with one click, or a plain resize, clearly labelled).
- Background jobs keep running across turns and restarts.
- AIs without image tools can ask a teammate for an image.
- Builder and reviewer suggestions based on each AI's record, plus time and retry stats.
- A project memory file every AI reads, with your rules in it.
- Several projects, each with its own board, chat and rules.
- Hearth logs can turn into tasks automatically (off until you turn it on).
- A phone view on your home network only (off until you turn it on, paired with a one-time code).
- Voice notes, transcribed on your own computer.
- Bigger Local AI models to choose from, each labelled for whether it fits your computer.

**The Habitat comes alive**
- Day and night, a break room, a garden that grows with finished work, visits when work changes hands, trophies, unlockable room props, a pet you can name, weather, a message board, moods and desk lights from real test results.
- **The AIs can live in it:** decorate their own room, take coffee breaks, water the garden, pet the pet and leave notes, and each keeps a journal so it remembers its room. You can lock any room and undo any change.

AppImage: update from inside the app (Settings → App updates → Check for updates). The .deb updates when you install the newer .deb.
