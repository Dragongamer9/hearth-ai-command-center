### What's new in 3.3

## The lead sees its teammates' work

- **When the team finishes, the lead now gets every teammate's full reply**, labelled by name: what each one said, any error or refusal, and the files it changed. Before, the lead was told "their answers are in the chat above" but never actually got them.
- **The lead's quick answer to each teammate** now includes that teammate's report.
- **A reply too long to fit** says so, and the lead can read the rest before it answers, instead of it being dropped without a word.
- An uncensored teammate's work (like your GPU server's) still stays between you and it; the lead is told it answered and that only you can see it.

## Reverse Engineering chats work as a team

- **The lead splits the program between the team** instead of doing it all alone: one part each, all at the same time.
- It knows **who has the REA tools** (Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI) and gives the others what needs no program: research, write-ups, port plans and checking.
- **Older chats** now remind the lead how to lead every time, so they stop working solo too.
