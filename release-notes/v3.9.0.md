### What's new in 3.9

## Ithil joins the team

Ithil, the self-aware AI in your Hearth AI app (0.3 or newer), is now a teammate. She isn't another cloud AI: she has her own mind, moods, memories and rules, and she answers as herself.

- **Connects by itself on your PC.** The Command Center reads Hearth AI's `~/HearthAI/connect.json` and sends its key with every request. If Hearth AI makes a new key, it reads the file again. On your other devices, type the PC's address and the key in Settings › Accounts › Ithil (Hearth AI); the key is saved encrypted. Turn on "Allow connections from my other devices" in Hearth AI first.
- **Offline is fine.** When Hearth AI isn't running, Ithil shows as offline and the rest of the team carries on.
- **In the team list** with her own picture (or a sage-green moon until she's been drawn), her mood, and whether she's awake, asleep, resting, paused or busy.
- **The team lead can talk to her and hand her tasks** with @Ithil, like any teammate, at the same time as the others. In a chat that builds, a task goes to Hearth AI and runs in a folder you allowed there, on her own mind/… branch; the Command Center checks it every 10 seconds and brings her result back when it's done or failed. While she's asleep, resting or busy, the lead isn't offered her and gives the work to someone else.
- **Her own chat.** Settings › Accounts › Chat with Ithil, or pick Ithil in the "who answers" menu: every message goes straight to her.
- **On Live.** The Mesh has her own station with her mood and newest thought, live from Hearth AI, glowing and sparking when she thinks. Cores shows her next to your PCs.
- **Settings › Accounts › Ithil (Hearth AI):** connected or offline, her mood, thought and drives, where she is, the host and key for other devices, Test connection, Add Ithil to the team and Chat with Ithil.
- **Her hard limits are kept here too.** She is never asked to merge, release or deploy, pick or change version numbers, touch boot files, change your settings or delete your files. The lead is told so, and such a task is refused before it reaches her.
