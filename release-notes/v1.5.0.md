### What's new in 1.5

**Switching works without restarting**
- Switching to a chat or project in another project now shows right away. Before, the window's safety rule against opening other pages also blocked the app's own reload, so a switch only showed after closing and reopening the app.

**Manage your chats**
- Hover a chat in the sidebar and click ⋯ to rename it, pin it to the top, move it up or down, or delete it. You can also drag chats to reorder them (within Pinned or Chats).
- Project team chats can be renamed too. They can't be deleted (their messages belong to the project), but you can hide one and show it again from "Hidden chats".
- Deleting asks first and moves the chat to "Recently deleted", with Undo right away and Restore later. Nothing is erased.
- With long lists, the sidebar now scrolls instead of squeezing rows together.

AppImage: update from inside the app (Settings → App updates → Check for updates).

.deb installs update from inside the app too (asks for your computer password once per update).
