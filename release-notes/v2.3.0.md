### What's new in 2.3

## Images & Videos works

- **Make it now sends what you typed.** In 2.2.1, pressing **Make it** always said to say what the picture or video should show, even with something typed in the box, so nothing could be made. Pictures and videos both start now.

Everything else from 2.2.1 is the same: Images & Videos with Wan 2.2 on your Nvidia PC, Uncensored mode in Settings, and each chat keeping its own settings.
