### What's new in 3.13

## Settings that fit on the screen

- **A compact Settings dialog.** It always fits the window; only its page scrolls, so nothing is cut off any more. A side list on the left (with Search settings) holds AI team, Accounts, Ithil, Tools, Memory, Voice & avatar and General.
- **One-line rows.** Each setting is a single row: its name, a short note, and the switch or button on the right. Groups sit in two columns when the window is wide.
- **Full forms open inside the dialog.** Buttons like ＋ Add, See and edit, Change… and Set up open the full page right there, with **‹ Back**.
- **AI team:** every AI in two columns with its switch, plus Team lead, Reviewer and what new messages start with, and Test team / Tool test.
- **Accounts** groups each AI by how it really connects: signed in with your account, with a key, your own, on the web, GitHub and your devices.

## Characters for every AI

- **Give any AI a 2D or 3D character.** Settings › Voice & avatar › Characters, then Change…: pick a starter (Ember fox, Robot pilot, Star twin, Wind sprite, Moss bear, Crystal), your own picture, one from your image maker, a Live2D model or a 3D VRM file.
- **It moves.** A live preview breathes, blinks and talks with the AI's voice (Happy, Thinking, Talking). The character stands in the right panel and the mini window when that AI answers.
- **Files stay on this PC**; nothing is sent to the AI. Ithil's body still always comes from Hearth AI.

## A right panel you can tuck away

- **⟨ collapses it** to a thin strip with the answering AI's face and buttons for read aloud, tools and Ithil.
- **▥ (or Ctrl + .) hides it** completely and brings it back. Open, collapsed or hidden is remembered.
